- Claude now tells you when Emet disputes a claim. After a turn ends, the plugin reads Emet's report on it, in the background. When the report is flagged, Claude speaks once more without being asked: it quotes each disputed claim, gives Emet's reason, and says that the check compares the answer only with evidence from that turn and can miss things or be wrong. It is asked not to say who is right, and to call no tool and open no link because of the report. This happens only when the report is ready within 60 seconds of the end of the turn and you have not started another turn, at most 3 times in a session, only in a session with a person at it, and only on Claude Code 2.1.287 or later. On an older Claude Code, and under `claude -p` or the SDK, turns are sent as before and no report is read. The extra message is a turn of your session and uses your Claude plan like any other. There is no setting that turns this off alone; disabling the plugin does. [claude/README.md](claude/README.md#when-emet-disputes-a-claim) has the details.
- Claude is never woken about the plugin's own commands. The answer to `/emet:status` or `/emet:setup` is sent to Emet as before, with nothing added, and its report is never read. `/emet:status` lists such a turn as `own_command`, under not checked. Running one of these commands counts as your next turn, so a report on the answer before it does not interrupt you.
- A second `Stop` hook, which Claude Code runs in the background (`async` and `asyncRewake`), does the reading. It sends nothing: it makes `GET` requests for the reports of turns the plugin sent, for 5 minutes at most after a turn ends.
- `/emet:status` lists the last five report checks under three headings: flagged, checked and not flagged, and not checked. A turn Emet found nothing against is worded as "no conflicts found with this turn's evidence". A report that was late, held back, failed or never read is listed as not checked or as flagged and not told, never as clean. Each entry shows its outcome word exactly as the plugin recorded it, then what the word means. The list shows ids, outcome words, times and the report link, and no text from a report. It is where you see a flagged report that Claude was not told about. A flagged report that was given to Claude with a later prompt is listed once, as `shown`, under flagged.
- The plugin keeps new files in its data folder: `state/<session>.checks.jsonl` and `state/<session>.stop.json` (ids, times and outcome words), `read-pause.json` (a time), and `verdicts/*.json`, one for each flagged report, which holds the text written for Claude about it, 4,000 characters at most, for 7 days. The report itself is never saved.
- `/emet:setup` says once what to expect from the report check, and that a report Claude could not be woken for comes with the next prompt.
- `status.json` has a `checks` list with the last five outcomes of the report check.
- `SECURITY.md` says how the text of a report is handled before Claude reads it.
