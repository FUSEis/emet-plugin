A turn that starts within a second or two of the end of the one before it, as a queued prompt or a background-task notice does, is no longer sent as finished while it still runs. It is now sent whole when it ends, and the report on its final answer is read. Before, the report on the first part of such a turn could be recorded as final, and a claim Emet disputed in the real answer was never shown. To tell such a turn apart, the session's state file now also keeps Claude Code's ids of the last 100 turns sent. They are never sent or logged.
