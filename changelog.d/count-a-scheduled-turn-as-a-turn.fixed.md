A turn that Claude Code starts on its own schedule (a `/loop` iteration, a scheduled wake-up) or for a message from another Claude session is now sent as a turn of its own, and its report is read like any other turn's. Before, such a turn was added to the trace of the turn before it and its answer was never checked. When such an entry comes right after a turn start that has no answer yet, such as a background-task notice, the two stay one turn, as before. Its prompt is sent under the same capture mode rules as any prompt, and no conversation title is taken from it. The plugin tells such a turn by fields of the session file that Claude Code 2.1.3xx writes and does not document (`isMeta` with `turnOrigin` of `scheduled` or `peer`).
