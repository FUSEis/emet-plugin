Every time the plugin writes in its own files is now in UTC with milliseconds, as in `2027-01-15T08:00:00.789Z`. This is the form Emet is already sent. Before, the log, `status.json` and the other files held the machine's local time in whole seconds, as in `2027-01-15T03:00:00-0500`, so `/emet:status` and `emet.log` now show UTC. Lines and files an earlier version wrote are still read.
